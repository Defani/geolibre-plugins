# iNaturalist Extractor

Fetch georeferenced iNaturalist observations by view or layer extent, with date, species, iconic-taxa, observer, keyword, quality-grade and advanced (taxon ID, place ID, photo license, project, annotation) filters, and a photo popup when available.

## Usage

1. Enable the **iNaturalist Extractor** plugin from the GeoLibre plugin panel.
2. Pan/zoom the map (or select a layer extent) to the area of interest.
3. Set filters — date range, species/taxon, observer, keyword, quality grade, and any advanced filters (taxon ID, place ID, photo license, project, annotation).
4. Click **Fetch** to load matching observations onto the map.
5. Click a point to view its details and photo (when available) in the popup.

## Credits, sources & references

- Observation data is retrieved from the [iNaturalist API](https://api.inaturalist.org/v1/docs/).
- iNaturalist and its data are provided by [iNaturalist.org](https://www.inaturalist.org/), a joint initiative of the California Academy of Sciences and the National Geographic Society.
- Individual observations, photos, and identifications remain the property of their respective observers and are subject to the license each observer has chosen on iNaturalist (see each observation's page for details).
